How the risk logic works

Low

Antivirus on, firewall on, fully updated, and a strong unique password. No action needed.

Medium

Exactly one protection is missing, outdated, or the password is weak. Fix the flagged item soon.

High

Two or more protections are missing/outdated, or the password is reused elsewhere. Fix immediately.
